Twitch account

Optional. With your login, streamlink plays ad-free if you have Turbo or a sub, and can open sub-only streams. Connecting reads only twitch.tv's auth-token cookie, hands it to the Puddock app on this computer (stored encrypted for your Windows account), and gives the cookie permission back right away. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
