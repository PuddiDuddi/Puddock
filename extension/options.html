Twitch account

Optional. With your login, streamlink plays without ads if you have Turbo or a sub to the channel, and opens sub-only streams. Connecting reads only twitch.tv's auth-token cookie, hands it to the Puddock app on this computer (kept in Windows Credential Manager), and gives the cookie permission back right away. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
